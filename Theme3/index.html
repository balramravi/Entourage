About

Operators, not passive money.

Jon C. WolfeFounder, Chairman & CEO

Jon founded Entourage in 2009 after more than twenty-five years running technology and operations inside the gaming industry. He built House Advantage, the Las Vegas loyalty platform behind HALo, into an international business, and Light & Wonder acquired its assets in 2022.

Today he invests in and builds technology and lifestyle companies, takes board seats where he can be useful, and gives the same attention to the charities the group supports.
