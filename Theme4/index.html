About

Jon C. Wolfe

Jon founded Entourage Investment Group in 2009. He previously founded House Advantage, whose assets Light & Wonder acquired in 2022, and brings 25+ years of leadership across casinos, technology and loyalty.

Today he invests in and builds technology and lifestyle companies, takes board seats where he can be useful, and gives the same attention to the charities the group supports.
